v1.25.1 — Add, then pack

Tap ＋ on a suggestion to add its quantity directly to your list, unpacked. Tap its circle to mark it packed. Existing stock is reused first; Undo reverses an accidental add. No extra form.

v1.25.0 — Your next outing

Trips is now home. Continue your active outing, collect gear by kit and shelf, keep departure items separate, then return gear and refill used supplies. My gear tracks ownership; trip checkmarks track packing. Shared stock cannot be packed twice across trips. Start with a description and add gear as you go. AI remains optional.

v1.24.0 — One FieldKit

Inventory, kits, trip packing and photo scanning share the same Notes-style lists, progress rings, packing circles, buttons and editors. Back returns to where you started; optional AI stays out of the everyday packing flow. Packed weights and failed-save indicators are corrected.

v1.23.0 — See where it lands

Hold the grip and drag: a live insertion gap shows before or after a row. The lifted label names the destination, and kit or bag targets highlight before you release.

v1.22.0 — Predictable swiping

Swipe to reveal buttons, then tap the action. Scrolling and canceled touches reset cleanly. One row opens at a time; tap outside to close it. Quantity and packing saves roll back on failure, and trip packing supports Undo.

v1.21.1: Fuel drag/drop into bags is blocked; outdoor storage stays protected.

v1.21.0 — Make it yours

Swipe cards for actions. Hold ⠿ and drag to reorder, move gear to a kit, or assign trip gear to a bag/person. Customize kits, trip details, missing supplies, departure presets and your own gear fields. Undo changes, and hide or restore cards without losing gear.

v1.20.0 — FieldKit in 3 taps

A short visual introduction shows how to count and locate gear, plan trips, and pack. Skip anytime; replay from More. Examples leave your inventory untouched.

v1.19.0 — Owned gear, smarter trips

Build a local trip kit from real stock, then optionally optimize with Gemini. Missing gear stays on a shopping list. Pack with full or partial checkmarks, or confirm photo matches. See weight by bag/person, protect edits, and review consumed supplies when finishing.

  • Plan a trip: describe where, how long and how many people. Build a kit from owned gear; review missing supplies separately. Optionally optimize with Gemini, then pack with checkmarks or photo confirmation.
  • Select several items and pack, unpack, add, remove, empty, move or archive them in one step, with Undo.
  • Scan & pack: lay your gear out, take one photo, and every item becomes a piece you can drag into a bag. Saving updates your inventory, with Undo.
  • The built-in finder cuts items out on your phone. Add a free Google key in More → Smart scan to name everything automatically.
  • Tap the circle to pack or unpack; kits show progress rings.

Your inventory, photos, notes and counts are preserved exactly as saved.

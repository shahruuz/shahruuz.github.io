v1.10.1 — Free public launch · October 17, 2026

The Alpine field guide

Deep forest green, warm sand and brass accents. Original mountain artwork, more distinctive headings, refined gear rows and a more polished mobile layout bring the app and website together. Your inventory and packing controls work just as before.

A complete first impression

A new product page introduces FieldKit with an interactive sample, practical explanations and a direct path into the free app. The app now shares its warmer field-journal design, with clearer controls and phone navigation.

More useful on packing day

  • Enter weights per item and see quantity-adjusted totals for each kit and the inventory.
  • Rename any of the six kits without changing where existing items are stored.
  • Show only empty items with the refill-list button.
  • Print a clean packing checklist from the inventory.
  • Get home-screen instructions and offline app-shell support in compatible browsers.

Your inventory comes first

The existing storage keys, quantities, notes, photos, Empty and Archive behavior remain. JSON exports now include kit names and weights. Import validation rejects partial or duplicate records, and a recovery snapshot is saved before imports and resets. Unreadable saved data is preserved for recovery.

Still simple

Free. No account. No payment. No automatic sync or AI recognition. Your inventory and photos stay in your browser until you export them.

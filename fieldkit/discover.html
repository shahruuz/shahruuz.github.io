GOOD TO KNOW

Travel light.
Know the details.

Is FieldKit really free?

Yes. This public release is free to use, with no account, payment details or subscription required.

Where does my inventory go?

It is saved in this browser on this device. Core planning stays on this device. Optional Google AI sends the details or photos you consent to share directly to Google. There is no automatic sync between devices. Export a backup regularly, especially before clearing browser storage.

Can I use it on my iPhone?

Yes, in your browser. In Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. If the installed app uses separate storage, import your exported JSON to bring your gear over.

Does a photo automatically identify my gear?

Optional photo scanning can help identify gear; Google AI scanning requires your own key and sharing consent. Review matches before saving. You can also attach a photo and enter or dictate the name yourself.

Can it guarantee that I’m ready for a trip?

No. The starter list is a suggestion, and weights include only what you enter. Check equipment, conditions, route, water, fit and emergency plans yourself before departure. Keep dedicated evacuation gear independent of shared trip supplies.

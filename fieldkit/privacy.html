FIELDKIT v1.13.0 · OCTOBER 17, 2026

Your gear stays yours.

FieldKit is a free browser app for organizing outdoor gear. No account or payment information is required.

What is saved

Item names, quantities, packing states, photos, weights, notes, kit names and archive records are saved in your browser’s local storage. App preferences and a recovery snapshot before a reset or import are also saved locally. The app does not transmit these records to a server.

Hosting and privacy

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting providers receive ordinary network requests, including IP addresses and browser information, when serving the site. FieldKit adds no analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, external fonts or third-party AI calls. Your app records are not part of the public website’s source code.

Back up before you move

Browser data can be lost when you clear site data, use private browsing, lose a device or run out of storage. There is no account-based recovery or automatic cloud sync. Open Backup → Download inventory regularly to save a JSON file containing your photos and kit names. CSV is a spreadsheet export and cannot restore photos or your full app state.

Importing replaces the local inventory after confirmation. A local recovery snapshot is created first; download it with Backup → Download recovery snapshot. The next import or reset replaces that snapshot. Download it elsewhere if you need to retain it. Empty JSON inventories are valid and preserve an intentionally cleared inventory.

Offline and installation

Visit online once before using offline. Browsers that support service workers can cache the app shell. This does not guarantee availability if the browser evicts cached files. In Safari on iPhone, choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Android browsers may offer Install app in the menu. Installation support varies. A home-screen app can use separate storage; export and import your inventory if needed.

Photos and voice

Photos are resized to reduce storage use and stay local unless you export or share them. A photo is not automatically identified. Keyboard dictation uses your device’s own keyboard service and its privacy settings. Do not put identity documents, security codes or other sensitive information in notes or photos.

Your checklist is a tool

Starter items are suggestions, begin at zero, and are not verified equipment. Confirm your route, weather, water, equipment condition, load and emergency plans yourself. Totals include only weights you have entered. Keep dedicated emergency gear separate from equipment borrowed for trips. FieldKit does not provide navigation, emergency alerts or a guarantee of safety.

Removing data

At zero, another minus archives an item. Archive offers Restore and Delete forever. Permanent deletion cannot be undone in the app; an older exported backup may still contain that item. Clearing site data in browser settings removes local records and offline files for the origin, which can affect other apps hosted on the same domain. Export first.

Need help or found a problem?

Use the public FieldKit issue form on GitHub. It may require a GitHub account. Describe the steps and browser, but do not attach your private inventory or sensitive photos to a public issue.

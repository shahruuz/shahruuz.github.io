6 zones
Storage: Use S1 fire starters, S2 batteries, S3 tools, S4 kitchen, S5 cleaning, and F for fuel cylinders outdoors. Store charging batteries and heat-sensitive supplies in a cool location.

Trail vs. evacuation: the waist bag and 10L module are for hiking problems (injury, delay, bad weather). The family evacuation kit is a different checklist. Don't rely on the 85L bag as something you can carry far, and never pack propane inside the bags.

How to send me new equipment

Lay 5–10 items on a clear table, take one overhead photo, and say something like: “Two flashlights, three battery packs, one torch; all in shared storage.” I can reconcile them with the exported inventory. For model numbers, expiry dates, or hard-to-identify objects, include one close-up photo.

Privacy: no data is uploaded from this site; even if this page is publicly hosted, gear records and photos remain in the local browser. Export only when you choose to share. Avoid saving sensitive IDs, alarm codes, or other private documents as photos here.
